Bing Live Wallpaper fetches the Bing image of the day at 11 UTC every day and sets it as your wallpaper.

Features:
* Scales the image to the largest screen dimension and centers it automatically.
* Redraws the image on screen layout change (e.g., screen rotation or unfolding foldables).
* Designed for folding phones: supports setting independent live wallpapers on inner and outer screens.
* Extremely lightweight with no background usage beyond the daily image fetch.
* No ads, no tracking, open source.
